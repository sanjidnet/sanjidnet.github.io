# Secrets to Optimal Client Service

1. **Don't use jargon** — makes clients look foolish and resent you

2. **Pause** — creates space for feedback and questions (resets power)

3. **Advise what is not in your interest** — creates credibility in the long run

4. **Ask open ended questions** — people enjoy talking about themselves

5. **Be upbeat** — the opposite will garner sympathy (nothing else)

6. **Don't mix business with social activity** — you are hired only because you are smart and you work hard

7. **Be humble** — make complimentary comments that indicate humility

8. **Be available / be responsive** — immediately

9. **Take a position** — don't equivocate, you are hired to tell them what to do!

10. **Control the meeting** — don't let anyone get you sidetracked

11. **Have an agenda** — communicate the agenda and enumerate, take notes in their presence (be a waiter), recap the follow up items to get buy-in; the opposite causes anxiety
